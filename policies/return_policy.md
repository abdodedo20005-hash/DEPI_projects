# Return & Warranty Policy

## General Return Policy

- Customers may return items within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- Items must be in their **original condition**: unopened, unused, with all original tags and packaging intact.
- A valid order ID or receipt is required for all returns.
- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** within 5 business days of receiving the returned item.

## Defective or Damaged Items

- If you receive a defective or damaged product, contact support within **7 days** of delivery.
- A prepaid return label will be provided.
- A full refund or replacement will be offered at no additional cost.

## Warranty

- All **electronic products** carry a **12‑month manufacturer warranty** from the date of purchase.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects only.
- **Not covered**: accidental damage, water damage, unauthorised repairs, or normal wear and tear.
- To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order ID and a description of the fault.

## Non‑Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned or exchanged:
- Items marked as **"Final Sale"** or clearance items.
- Customised or personalised products.
- Perishable goods.
- Opened software, digital downloads, or consumables.

## Shipping Costs

- Standard returns (change of mind): the customer covers return shipping.
- Defective/warranty returns: ShopAgent covers shipping.

## Contact

For all return and warranty queries, please contact **support@shopagent.example.com** or use the chat assistant.
